### Format & Structure of the LLD Round



The Locus interview loop for backend/senior roles includes: 1 coding round, **1 LLD take-home**, 1 HLD interview, and a culture fit round.

 This means the LLD round at Locus is **take-home style** — you complete a design assignment offline and then walk through it.



Coding and design rounds are outsourced to 3rd-party interviewers.

 This means your LLD evaluator is likely not a Locus engineer but an external panel.



The take-home problem has multiple stages — after passing all test cases of one stage, the next part unlocks. It tests both coding ability and design thinking. A detailed design statement is given, followed by MCQs based on that statement.

### Themes & Patterns

- **Domain alignment is deliberate.** Locus is a logistics/route optimization company. The Google Maps LLD question directly mirrors their core product (routing, geocoding, multi-stop planning). Expect your LLD problem to be in the logistics/maps/delivery domain.
- **Take-home format, not live whiteboard.** Unlike companies that give you 45 mins live, Locus's LLD is a take-home assignment — you have more time but are expected to produce working, well-structured code.
- **Staged problem unlocking.** The multi-stage format means your code must actually pass test cases to proceed — it's not just about class diagrams; working code matters.
- **3rd-party evaluators.** Because coding/design rounds are outsourced, the rubric may emphasize SOLID principles, clean code, and extensibility (standard 3rd-party LLD evaluation criteria) rather than Locus-specific domain depth.
