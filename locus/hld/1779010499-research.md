### Round Format & Structure



The Locus SDE interview loop for senior/SDE3 roles consists of: **1 coding round, 1 LLD take-home, 1 HLD interview, and a culture fit round.**





Notably, coding and design rounds are outsourced to 3rd-party interviewers.

 This means the HLD interviewer may not be an internal Locus engineer.



All rounds are typically completed in a single day, with offers sometimes rolled out the same evening.
