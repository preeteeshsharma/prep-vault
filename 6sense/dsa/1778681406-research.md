Key observations:
- 

At the Senior level, candidates face **two dedicated DSA rounds** (60 min each), one typically covering DP and one covering strings/algorithms.


- 

For more junior-track rounds, difficulty skews easy-to-medium LeetCode — one with good DSA practice can solve them easily.


- 

For senior roles, coding questions range up to LeetCode medium and hard, but interviewers give hints toward the optimal solution.



---

### Preparation Tips (from candidates)

- 

Interviewers assess problem-solving abilities, coding proficiency, and understanding of data structures and algorithms — be prepared to explain your thought process and approach to solving complex problems.


- Focus heavily on **DFS/BFS on graphs and 2D grids** — this appears across the OA, phone screen, and onsite.
- For Senior roles, make sure you can not only solve DP problems but also **reconstruct/print the path** (e.g., the LCS backtracking variant).
- **Palindrome substrings** at a non-trivial level (counting specific types) is tested — review Manacher's algorithm and expand-around-center approaches.
