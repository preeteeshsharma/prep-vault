## Interview Loop: 6sense — Senior Software Engineer

> Data compiled from 6+ sources across Glassdoor, Blind, EnginEBogie, LeetCode Discuss, and InterviewQuery. Most recent: Oct 2025. Search date: May 13, 2026.

---

### Overview

- **Rounds**: 5–6 total (recruiter screen → online assessment OR phone screen → coding rounds × 2 → system design(s) × 1–2 → behavioral/HM round)
- **Total duration**: 

Candidates applying for Software Engineer roles take an average of **20 days** to get hired. The hiring process at 6sense overall averages 24.3 days.

 Individual reports range from 3 days to 3 months depending on the team and location.
- **Format**: Fully virtual (Zoom); some older accounts mention onsite
- **Difficulty**: 

Candidates give an average difficulty score of **3 out of 5** (where 5 is highest) for their job interview at 6sense.


- **Recency of data**: 2020–Oct 2025 (most actionable data: 2022–2025)

---

### Round-by-Round Breakdown

---

#### Round 1 — Recruiter / HR Screen
- **Duration**: ~30 min
- **Format**: Phone or video call
- 

The process begins with an initial phone screening, usually conducted by a recruiter, focusing on your background, the role you're applying for, and your fit within the company culture.


- 

Expect to discuss your previous experiences, technical skills, and motivations for wanting to join 6sense.



---

#### Round 2 — Online Coding Assessment (OA) *or* Phone Technical Screen
- **Duration**: ~60–180 min
- **Format**: HackerRank (timed) or live phone/Zoom coding
- Two distinct patterns appear across candidates:

  **Pattern A — HackerRank OA:**
  

The first step is a HackerRank test of 3 hours, consisting of a medium-hard question on DFS. The result is conveyed within a day.



  **Pattern B — Live phone technical screen:**
  

A recruiter reached out on LinkedIn and scheduled a short initial phone screen; next was a 1-hour coding interview.



  One Blind candidate confirmed a separate system design phone screen before the onsite: 

After clearing the system design phone interview, they had an onsite scheduled with another system design and 3 coding rounds.



---

#### Round 3 — DSA / Coding Round 1
- **Duration**: ~60 min
- **Format**: Live coding, Zoom
- 

This round focused on a dynamic programming problem, specifically a variant of the Longest Common Subsequence (LCS) algorithm, followed by a request to print the path.


- 

Coding questions are LeetCode medium and hard, but interviewers give hints toward the optimal solution.


- Topics that appear: DFS, BFS, binary search, dynamic programming, graphs, string manipulation

---

#### Round 4 — DSA / Coding Round 2
- **Duration**: ~60 min
- **Format**: Live coding, Zoom
- 

This round involved a string manipulation problem focused on identifying and counting specific types of palindromic substrings.


- Confirmed across multiple candidates: 

A final round with 3 programming interviews and 1 behavioral interview was typical. Coding questions were LeetCode medium and hard.



---

#### Round 5 — High-Level System Design (HLD)
- **Duration**: ~60 min
- **Format**: Zoom whiteboard/discussion
- 

This round focused on the high-level design of a real-time messaging platform, covering aspects like scalability and core features.


- For a Staff-level role on LeetCode Discuss (Oct 2025), the HLD round involved: 

a system design question around executing long-running workflows like Apache Airflow with a modification that certain parts of those workflows should be executed once at a given time in day.


- One Blind user confirmed the onsite includes 

another system design and 3 coding rounds

 after clearing the phone system design screen.

---

#### Round 6 — Low-Level Design (LLD) *and/or* Behavioral / HM Round
- **Duration**: ~60 min each
- **Format**: Zoom

  **LLD (confirmed for senior-level):**
  

This round focused on the low-level design of a multi-level parking lot, requiring a detailed class design.



  **Behavioral / Hiring Manager:**
  

One Glassdoor candidate described: (1) HM round (30 min) covering experience and behavioral questions; (2) System design (1 hour, easy to medium difficulty); (3) Final 2 coding rounds (1 hour each, medium to hard difficulty).



  

Behavioral interviews focus on past experiences, teamwork, and how you handle challenges. Interviewers look for evidence of communication skills, leadership potential, and alignment with 6sense's core values.

### Red Flags & Insider Notes

- **Ghosting after final rounds** is a documented pattern. 

One 2022 candidate reported the whole process took around 1 month, but they never got the results of the final interview — no rejection or acceptance email, calling it "unprofessional."


- **Round count varies by team.** 

One senior-level candidate confirmed 6 total rounds.

 Other candidates describe as few as 4. Don't assume a fixed number.
- **LLD is a senior-specific round.** It appears specifically in senior and above accounts, not in SWE IC1/IC2 reports.
- 

Interviews at 6sense can be conversational — interviewers share constructive feedback. Asking insightful questions about the team, projects, and company direction is encouraged.


- **Interviewers may give hints.** Multiple candidates confirmed that on hard problems, interviewers nudge toward optimal solutions rather than expecting you to independently reach them.
