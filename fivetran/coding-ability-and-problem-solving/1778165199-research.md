### Round Format & Platform



Fivetran uses two distinct coding challenges. The first — "Problem Solving" — is a fairly standard coding question designed to determine a base level of problem-solving ability in the language of your choice, administered via HackerRank.





The second challenge, "Coding Structure and Data Processing," is a more focused problem designed to evaluate your ability to interact with a codebase, think structurally, and perform basic data manipulation — given as part of the on-site, in Java (though interviewers are instructed to assist with minor language difficulties for non-Java engineers).





The live technical interview round typically lasts one hour and is divided into two parts, both focused on data structures and algorithms.





The technical interview process consists of discussing, estimation (Big O notation), improving or simplifying, coding, checking, and a detailed discussion of the pros and cons of your decision.
